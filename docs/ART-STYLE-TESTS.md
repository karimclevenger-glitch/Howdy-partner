# Art Style Tests: prompts to try in ChatGPT

These are **experiments, not final art.** They exist so we can choose a look together and find out early
what ChatGPT is good and bad at for this project. Nothing you make here is wasted: if you love a result, it can
become the reference for the real thing.

**How to do it**
1. Open a new ChatGPT chat and paste one prompt at a time.
2. If you like where an image is going but want changes, just tell ChatGPT ("make her coat more purple",
   "less cluttered"). That's normal and expected.
3. Paste your favorites (and any you hate) back into our chat, with your gut reaction. "I don't know why but
   I love this one" is genuinely useful feedback.

These prompts deliberately **don't name any existing studio, film or game.** We describe the style in our own
words so the result is ours.

## Test 1: Background, Style A (Painted Storybook)

> Wide landscape illustration to be used as a background in a point-and-click adventure game. Landscape format, 3:2.
>
> Scene: a vast stormy sea that has turned completely to amber glass in a single instant. Giant waves are frozen
> mid-curl, and spray hangs in the air as glittering glass droplets. A low golden-hour sun shines through the glass
> waves so they glow honey-gold and sea-glass teal from inside. In the middle distance, an old wooden galleon is
> caught half-sunk and tilted inside a frozen wave. Far off on the left, a small striped lighthouse stands dark on a
> reef of glass. On the far horizon, a cliffside shore.
>
> Composition: this is a stage for characters. The lower third of the image is a broad, fairly flat frozen wave crest,
> smooth glass that characters could stand and walk on, running left to right. Keep the top 12% and bottom 12% free of
> important details because they may be cropped. No characters, no people, no animals.
>
> Style: hand-painted storybook illustration in gouache and watercolor, soft visible brush texture, rich warm light,
> gentle ink linework on the major shapes, whimsical fairy-tale atmosphere. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no border.

## Test 3: Character, Beatrix ("Granny Trix")

> Character design for a 2D adventure game. One character only, full body, standing in a relaxed neutral pose,
> three-quarter view, the whole figure visible from head to toe with empty space around her.
>
> Character: Beatrix, a 72-year-old retired adventurer who is also a dowager queen. Small and wiry, with a mischievous,
> sharp, amused expression and deep laugh lines. Silver hair in a messy bun with a pencil stuck through it. Round
> wire spectacles. A small gold circlet worn slightly crooked. A long weathered traveling coat in deep plum with too many
> pockets, over a practical dark skirt and sturdy old leather boots. She leans on a carved wooden walking cane with a
> brass hook-shaped handle. A small brass ear-trumpet hangs from her belt on a cord.
>
> Style: clean bold outlines, flat colors with one soft shade per color, the look of a classic hand-animated fairy-tale
> film character, expressive face, slightly stylized proportions (head a little large, which reads well on small screens).
> Plain flat light-grey background, no shadow on the ground. No text, no labels, no watermark.

**Follow-up in the same chat:**

> Now show this exact same character from the side, facing right, and from the back. Same style, same colors,
> same proportions, side by side on the same plain light-grey background.

*(This follow-up is the real test. We're checking whether ChatGPT keeps her looking like the same person.)*

## Test 4: Character, Tamsin

> Character design for a 2D adventure game. One character only, full body, standing in a relaxed neutral pose,
> three-quarter view, the whole figure visible from head to toe with empty space around her.
>
> Character: Tamsin, a 17-year-old princess who would much rather be an adventurer. Tall and a bit gangly, bright
> curious eyes, freckles, an eager half-smile. Dark curly hair tied back with a teal ribbon, with strands escaping.
> A charcoal pencil tucked behind one ear. A practical mustard-yellow dress hitched up over dark leggings, scuffed
> brown boots, a short teal cape. A worn old leather satchel slung across her body, with a sketchbook poking out of it.
>
> Style: clean bold outlines, flat colors with one soft shade per color, the look of a classic hand-animated fairy-tale
> film character, expressive face, slightly stylized proportions (head a little large). Plain flat light-grey
> background, no shadow on the ground. No text, no labels, no watermark.

> **I've left skin tone and ethnicity out of both characters on purpose.** Add whatever matches the picture in your
> head. They're grandmother and granddaughter, so they should look plausibly related. If you leave it out,
> ChatGPT will pick for you, and it may not pick what you'd pick.

## Bonus Test 5: Puppet pieces (the important one)

This tells us whether my cut-out animation plan will work with ChatGPT. Do it in the **same chat** as Test 3,
after you have a Beatrix you like.

> Using the character above as an exact reference, create a cut-out puppet kit of her for 2D animation, side view
> facing right. Draw each body part as a separate piece, spaced well apart on a plain flat light-grey background,
> with no pieces touching: the head (with hair, spectacles and circlet), the torso with coat (shoulders to hips),
> one upper arm, one forearm with hand, one thigh, one lower leg with boot, the walking cane on its own, and the
> ear-trumpet on its own. Each limb piece should have rounded ends at the joints, like a paper puppet pinned at the
> shoulders, elbows, hips and knees, so the pieces can rotate without showing gaps. Same outlines, colors and
> proportions as the reference. No text, no labels.

Don't worry if it comes out messy. I want to see *how* it's messy, because that tells me which animation approach
to build.

## What I'll look at when you send these back

- **Consistency:** does Beatrix still look like Beatrix from the side and back?
- **Clean edges:** are the outlines crisp enough to cut out and animate?
- **Image size:** what size does ChatGPT actually give us? (I'll check the files.)
- **The walkable floor:** did the background leave a clear stage for characters to walk on?
- **Your gut:** which one feels like the game you've been imagining since you were a kid?
